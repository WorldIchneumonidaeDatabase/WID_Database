# PanelEtymology (`panel:etymology`)

Overview tab, genus and species pages. Title "Name: gender, form and etymology".

- The **name** itself, large (the epithet for species, the whole name for genera), with **pills**
  for its grammar from the name's "Latinized" classifications: gender (blue) and part of speech
  (green: adjective, participle, noun in apposition, noun in genitive case).
- One line saying **what it means**: nouns keep their ending whatever the genus; adjectives and
  participles agree with the genus; a genus's gender sets the endings of adjectival species names.
- For adjectives and participles, the **masculine, feminine and neuter forms**.
- The **etymology** (Markdown) in a highlighted box.

Data: the taxon of the page and `/taxon_name_classifications?taxon_name_id[]=`. Hidden when there
is nothing to show. The same panel, restyled, is on Syrphidae of Italy.
